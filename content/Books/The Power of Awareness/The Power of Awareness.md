# The Power of Awareness

Read *The Power of Awareness* by Neville Goddard online. The book explores consciousness and assumption through short chapters on desire, attention, persistence and related themes. Its chapter structure makes it useful for returning to one question at a time.

Begin with the opening chapters for the argument behind the practices. To compare the themes before reading, open the [summary and chapter guide](/summaries/the-power-of-awareness/). For practical companions, see [living in the end](/techniques/living-in-the-end/) and [mental diet](/techniques/mental-diet/).
