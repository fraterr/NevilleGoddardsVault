# Feeling is the Secret

Read *Feeling Is the Secret* by Neville Goddard online, chapter by chapter. This short book develops his account of consciousness, feeling, sleep and prayer. It is a useful starting point if you want to understand the ideas behind imagining a fulfilled desire before sleep.

Start with the first chapter below and read in order. For a quick overview, use the [Feeling Is the Secret summary](/summaries/feeling-is-the-secret/). The [SATS guide](/techniques/sats/) offers practical context and links to passages in the collection; [SATS success stories](/success-stories/sats/) contains separate, self-reported community accounts.

This introduction and the linked summary are editorial reading aids. The chapter links below open the book text hosted in the Vault.
