# Neville Goddard Books: Read Online

Explore the books in the Vault and read them chapter by chapter. Each book has a contents list, and the [book summaries](/summaries/) offer another way to compare their main ideas before choosing what to read.

## Which Neville Goddard book should I read first?

For a short introduction, begin with [Feeling Is the Secret](/books/feeling-is-the-secret/). It connects feeling, sleep and prayer in a compact account of Neville's teaching. Read it alongside the [SATS guide](/techniques/sats/) if your interest is imaginal practice before sleep.

For a more structured exploration, choose [The Power of Awareness](/books/the-power-of-awareness/). Its short chapters address assumption, attention, desire and persistence. The [summary and chapter guide](/summaries/the-power-of-awareness/) can help you return to a particular question.

For imagination and revision, try [Awakened Imagination](/books/awakened-imagination/). For Neville's interpretation of Biblical language, explore [Your Faith Is Your Fortune](/books/your-faith-is-your-fortune/).

## Reading for a practical question

- **Sleep and imaginal scenes:** [Feeling Is the Secret](/books/feeling-is-the-secret/) and [SATS](/techniques/sats/).
- **Attention and habitual assumptions:** [The Power of Awareness](/books/the-power-of-awareness/) and [mental diet](/techniques/mental-diet/).
- **Revisiting an experience:** [Awakened Imagination](/books/awakened-imagination/) and [revision](/techniques/revision/).
- **Accounts of applying the teaching:** [The Law and the Promise](/books/the-law-and-the-promise/) and the separate collection of [Reddit success stories](/success-stories/).
