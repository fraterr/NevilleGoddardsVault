# Five Lessons

In 1948, Neville Goddard taught a five-night class in Los Angeles that has become the most sought-after distillation of his entire teaching. Each evening he delivered one lesson — a complete, practical statement of the law and its application — to a class of students he challenged to test everything before the week was out.

The five lessons build on one another:

1. **Consciousness Is the Only Reality** — the foundation, and the technique of prayer given in full.
2. **Assumptions Harden Into Fact** — the Bible read psychologically, and the power of the assumed state.
3. **Thinking Fourth-Dimensionally** — time, the future occupied now, and the mechanics of the imaginal act.
4. **No One to Change But Self** — the mirror of consciousness and the daily practice of the drowsy state.
5. **Remain Faithful to Your Idea** — persistence, and the closing counsel to walk as though you were it.

These transcripts are reproduced from the widely circulated public-domain text of the 1948 class.
