# Awakened Imagination

Read *Awakened Imagination* by Neville Goddard online. The book explores imagination, inner speech and revision, including the chapter “The Pruning Shears of Revision.” It connects his account of inner experience with practices readers can study in the text.

Use the [Awakened Imagination summary](/summaries/awakened-imagination/) for a guide to the themes, or begin with the first chapter below. The [revision guide](/techniques/revision/) explains the practice with source links. The separate [revision success stories](/success-stories/revision/) page collects personal Reddit accounts and questions to help you compare them.

The introduction and linked summary are editorial companions to the hosted book text.
