# Your Faith Is Your Fortune

Read *Your Faith Is Your Fortune* by Neville Goddard online. This book presents his interpretation of consciousness, the words “I AM” and Biblical stories as descriptions of inner experience. It is a useful reading choice if you want to understand the religious language used throughout his teaching.

Read the chapters in sequence or consult the [Your Faith Is Your Fortune summary](/summaries/your-faith-is-your-fortune/) to locate a theme. The [I AM guide](/techniques/i-am/) and [Bible reference index](/search/bible-references/) offer ways to compare passages across the collection.
