This site began with a simple frustration. Neville Goddard's books and lectures are everywhere online, and yet finding exactly what he said, and where he said it, is strangely hard. Quotes circulate without sources. Techniques get explained third-hand. The texts themselves sit in scattered archives with no way to search across them.

The Vault is an attempt to fix that. The principle behind everything here is that claims should be anchored to their source. Every technique guide, every book summary and every quotation on this site links to the exact passage in Neville's own words, and the full texts are here too: free, searchable and readable.

## Where to start

If you are new to Neville, the [Start Here path](/start-here) walks you through the essential readings over 21 days, each paired with a daily practice.

If you struggle to believe any of this can work, begin with [Test the Law](/test-the-law) instead: seven low-stakes experiments, starting with the famous ladder.

If you want the ideas fast, the [Book Summaries](/summaries) give you each book in about three minutes, with every chapter linked.

And when doubt creeps in, read a few of the [Success Stories](/success-stories). Hundreds of first-hand reports from the community, organized by goal, technique and how long it took.

## A living archive

The Vault keeps growing. You can now highlight passages and keep private notes on every text, new guides appear as they are written, and this blog is where I will share study notes, site updates and reflections on the practice.

Everything here is free, without ads or signups. If the Vault serves you, share it with someone who needs it. Better yet, practice.
