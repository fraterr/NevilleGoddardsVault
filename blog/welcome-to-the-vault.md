This site began with a simple frustration: Neville Goddard's books and lectures are everywhere online, and yet finding *exactly* what he said — and where he said it — is strangely hard. Quotes circulate without sources. Techniques are explained third-hand. The texts themselves sit in scattered archives with no way to search across them.

The Vault is an attempt to fix that, built on one principle: **everything anchored to the source**. Every technique guide, every book summary, every claim on this site links to the exact passage in Neville's own words — and the full texts are here, free, searchable, and readable.

## Where to start

- If you are new to Neville, the [Start Here path](/start-here) walks you through the essential readings over 21 days, each paired with a daily practice.
- If you struggle to believe any of this can work, begin with [Test the Law](/test-the-law) — seven low-stakes experiments, starting with the famous ladder.
- If you want the ideas fast, the [Book Summaries](/summaries) give you each book in three minutes, with every chapter linked.
- And when doubt creeps in, the [Success Stories](/success-stories) — hundreds of first-hand reports from the community — are the best medicine I know.

## A living archive

The Vault keeps growing: highlights and private notes on every text, new guides, and now this blog — where I'll share study notes, site updates, and reflections on the practice.

Everything here is free, without ads or signups. If the Vault serves you, the best ways to give back are to share it with someone who needs it — and to practice.
